# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-07

### Added

- Added 6×6 and 9×9 Factory Gauges for configuring Create Mechanical Crafting recipes with packages.
- Added automatic recipe arrangement that preserves empty cells in larger crafting patterns.
- Added Portuguese and English names, tooltips, and Ponder scenes for the new gauges.
- Added distinct Brass and Industrial Iron colors for the expanded gauge panels.
- Added recipes to upgrade two Factory Gauges into an Expanded gauge, and two Expanded gauges into an Industrial gauge.
