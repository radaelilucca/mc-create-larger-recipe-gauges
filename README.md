<p align="center" style="text-align: center;">An addon for <b>Create</b></p>

<p align="center" style="background-color: #F4ECD0; text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radaelilucca/mc-create-larger-recipe-gauges/main/docs/gallery/section-titles/what_it_is.png" alt="WHAT IT IS?" width="267"></p>

Create's **Factory Gauge** is built around a 3×3 recipe grid. That limits the Mechanical Crafting recipes you can automate with packages, even when your factory has room for a larger crafter array.

**Create: Larger Recipe Gauges** adds two upgradeable gauges: the **Expanded Factory Gauge** for recipes up to 6×6 and the **Industrial Factory Gauge** for recipes up to 9×9. They use Create's existing Factory Gauge block and interface, so you can set up package-based Mechanical Crafting with larger recipes.

---

<p align="center" style="background-color: #F4ECD0; text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radaelilucca/mc-create-larger-recipe-gauges/main/docs/gallery/section-titles/what_can_it_do.png" alt="WHAT CAN IT DO?" width="375"></p>

Larger gauges let you automate larger Mechanical Crafting recipes through Create's package delivery system.

<p align="center" style="text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radaelilucca/mc-create-larger-recipe-gauges/main/docs/gallery/crushing-wheel.png" alt="Crushing Wheel recipe configured in a Factory Gauge" width="49%"></p>

### Expanded Factory Gauge

**Configure Mechanical Crafting recipes up to 6×6.** Craft it by combining two Create Factory Gauges; the recipe is shapeless.

- Use the same gauge interface to choose an output and arrange recipe ingredients.
- It keeps Create's original Factory Gauge available with its original behavior.

<p align="center" style="text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radaelilucca/mc-create-larger-recipe-gauges/main/docs/gallery/potato-cannon.png" alt="Potato Cannon recipe configured in a Factory Gauge" width="49%"></p>

### Industrial Factory Gauge

**Configure Mechanical Crafting recipes up to 9×9.** Craft it by combining two Expanded Factory Gauges; the recipe is shapeless.

- Use it for larger patterns, including recipes with empty spaces between ingredients.
- Your connected Mechanical Crafter array can be larger than the recipe pattern.

---

<p align="center" style="background-color: #F4ECD0; text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radaelilucca/mc-create-larger-recipe-gauges/main/docs/gallery/section-titles/how-do-i-use-it.png" alt="HOW DO I USE IT?" width="371"></p>

1. **Craft a gauge:** combine two Create Factory Gauges for an Expanded Factory Gauge, or two Expanded Factory Gauges for an Industrial Factory Gauge.
2. **Set up the recipe:** choose the output item in the gauge and connect ingredient gauges. Open the recipe settings and enable automatic arrangement for a matching Mechanical Crafting recipe.
3. **Build and connect the crafters:** assemble a powered Mechanical Crafter array large enough for the recipe. Use a wrench to connect the crafters from behind.
4. **Deliver packages:** attach a Packager to the connected crafters, set a delivery address in the gauge, and route packages with that address to the Packager.

> **Heads up:** the crafter array does not need to match the recipe's exact shape. A 9×9 array can craft a 5×5 recipe, and empty recipe cells stay empty when packages are unpacked.

The mod expands Factory Gauge recipe capacity; it does not add new crafting machines or change the original gauge's interface.

Install the mod on both the client and server, alongside **Create**.

- **Minecraft 1.21.1** with **NeoForge 21.1.236** and **Create 6.0.10**
- **Minecraft 1.20.1** with **Forge 47.4.0** and **Create 6.0.8**
